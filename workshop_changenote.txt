v1.2.0 - New map look from Athena Web 2 + fixes

New
- Map rendering from Athena Web 2: classic Athena colours, smoothed coastlines and contour lines, airport surfaces, tree markers and the classic Athena vehicle silhouettes
- Collapsible sidebars (< and > buttons) and a tablet layout
- I-TGT target capture: press T or the middle mouse button (SAVE TGT on a tablet) to store a target
- Follow Player switches off when you pan or zoom yourself

Fixes
- No map while Arma is in the main menu or the editor (welcome screen until a mission runs)
- Exported maps are kept in Documents\AthenaRemastered\MapCache, so they are not exported again after an update (existing exports are copied over automatically)
- Forest overlay no longer far too dense; with an Athena Desktop export it matches Athena Web 2
- Every shot appeared twice in Recent Shots
- Crew labels showed "P:" for everyone; they now show D: / G: / C:
- Drones, turrets and radars no longer show as a large tilted rectangle
- M5 Sandstorm / artillery: the projectile path uses the real impact point and time of flight after the first impact
- "Loaded from cache" box disappears after a few seconds; version label no longer cut off

Known limitations
- Without a laze, the first artillery/rocket shot of a fire mission is still a fixed-range guess (real in-flight tracking is planned)
- Map styles Ground and Pilot are being reworked

BattlEye
- The extension DLL is unchanged and not allowlisted: use -noBE for singleplayer / LAN
